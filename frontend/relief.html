Relief Distribution Schedule

Verified supply distribution points, dry ration drops, and medical aid schedules for the ongoing emergency.

📦 ASDMA & Indian Red Cross Multi-Agency Relief Coordination

Relief materials are supplied completely free of charge under State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) norms. District administrations across all 15 affected districts are operating daily distribution shifts. Special NDRF boat convoys and IAF air-drops are prioritized for marooned river island (char) communities.

📋 Standard Family Ration Scale (Per Week)

  • Rice: 12 kg per family (or 3 kg chira/flattened rice)
  • Dal (Lentils): 2 kg
  • Salt: 1 kg iodized salt
  • Mustard Cooking Oil: 1 Litre
  • Baby Nutrition / Milk Powder: 500g (for families with infants)

🧪 Health & Hygiene Protection Pack

  • Halogen Tablets: 50 tablets (1 tablet per 10L clean water)
  • ORS Sachets: 10 WHO-standard sachets
  • Antiseptic Soap & Bleach: 2 soap bars + bleaching powder packet
  • Sanitary Napkins: 2 packs per household
  • LLIN Mosquito Nets: 2 medicated bed nets

Marooned on an Inundated Char Island?

Spread an orange tarpaulin or white cloth on the highest dry rooftop/mound for IAF helicopter air-drop pilots to spot your location, and call the State Disaster Control Room.
