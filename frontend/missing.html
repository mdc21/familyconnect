How your report is handled

  • A person and a case are kept separate — several reports can describe the same person.
  • Your relationship is recorded as a request for access, decided by a coordinator.
  • Any second report by another family member is reconciled, not silently overwritten.
  • FamilyConnect cannot confirm identity or death. Those remain with the authorities.

DNA identification

Where remains were buried before identification, a close relative's DNA reference sample can still provide an answer. You can apply for a sample kit and track it without an account.
