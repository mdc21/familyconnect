ℹ️ Operational Context: Mountain Tunnel SAR vs. Assam Plain Flooding

Hydropower tunnel rescue operations and horizontal micro-drilling tracking are active for the Nepal–Tibet Border Flash Flood event (Upper Trishuli 3A and Rasuwagadhi Hydropower tunnels). In the Assam Brahmaputra River Basin Floods, emergency search and rescue is conducted by NDRF and SDRF via shallow-draft rescue boats across inundated chars (river islands) and flooded villages.
