For authorities and partner organisations

FamilyConnect is a coordination layer for the family-facing side of the response. It does not replace your systems, your authority, or your decisions — it makes your verified information reachable by the families who need it.

Your authority is never transferred

Every submission is attributed to your organization by name, carries your internal reference number, and remains subject to your verification. Nothing is merged or confirmed by a computer alone.

Core Principles for Responding Agencies

1. One verified channel instead of many unverified ones

Your updates are published once, with your organisation named as the source, your own reference number, and a timestamp. Families see them next to every unverified claim, clearly labelled — which reduces repeated phone calls and duplicated reports.

2. Structured case data, not ad-hoc messages

Missing person reports, safe declarations and hospital admission updates arrive in a consistent format with the fields coordinators actually need. Nothing is merged or matched automatically — staff review every suggestion.

3. Rumour control with your authority behind it

When false claims circulate about casualty numbers, road access or rescue status, coordinators publish counter-statements that cite the responsible authority by name. Confirmed information is never attributed to FamilyConnect itself.

4. Minimum data, clear accountability

The platform holds only the information needed to locate and assist people. Every read and change by staff is recorded in an immutable audit trail you can ask to review.

How Onboarding Works for Agencies

  1. 1. Register your organisation

    Submit your organisation name, role in the response (police, hospital, NGO, embassy, tour operator), and an official contact. Administrators verify credentials before granting access.

  2. 2. Nominate authorised users

    Each user receives an individual partner account linked to your organisation. There are no shared logins, and every submission is attributed to a named responder.

  3. 3. Submit updates with your own internal reference

    Partners submit information against a case with a source type and your internal case/hospital reference number. Submissions are verified before broadcast.

  4. 4. Strict scoping & privacy control

    Partner accounts see only the cases they are authorized to access. Safeguarding and restricted case notes are strictly protected by role-based access control.

DNA Reference Samples from Families

Where remains are buried or hard to identify, relatives can apply to give a DNA reference sample. Direct families to the Family DNA Request form. Comparisons are sorted by similarity for human review; only your authority can confirm a match.
