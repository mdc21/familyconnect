How to find a family member after a glacial outburst flood

When a flood or debris flow disrupts power, mobile networks and roads across Himalayan valleys, finding a relative requires checking several channels systematically.

Systematic search steps

Follow these steps to trace a missing family member, coordinate with on-the-ground responders, and stay informed without duplicating efforts.

  1. 1. Check safety declarations first

    Survivors who reach reception desks or mobile coverage areas use FamilyConnect to declare themselves safe. Search the public safety list or check if your relative has submitted an update.

  2. 2. Register a missing person report early

    Filing a report creates a single tracking reference used by Nepal Police, NRCS caseworkers, and hospital teams across Rasuwa, Pokhara, and Kathmandu.

  3. 3. Visit physical Family Assistance Centres

    In-person assistance desks are operating 24 hours at Bhrikutimandap Exhibition Ground in Kathmandu and Rasuwagadhi Border Reception Desk. Staff provide interpretation, lodging referral, and direct caseworker checks.

  4. 4. Coordinate with consular channels for foreign national relatives

    If your missing relative is a foreign trekker or national, contact their embassy or consulate desk at TIA Arrivals Hall Desk 4. Embassies cross-reference tour group manifests and hospital admissions.

  5. 5. Request a reference DNA sample kit if advised

    Where recovered remains cannot be identified visually due to mudslide trauma, close relatives (parents, children, siblings) can request a DNA sample kit to enable laboratory matching.
