Foreign national missing: embassy and consular help

If the missing person is an international trekker, tourist, pilgrim, or humanitarian worker, consular missions provide vital coordination between foreign families and local Nepalese emergency services.

Role of Consular Missions

Embassies advocate with statutory authorities, facilitate medical & repatriation logistics, and verify official documents. They do not conduct independent search and rescue operations on the mountain.

  1. 1. What embassies CAN do

    Liaise directly with Nepal Police and Nepali Army search operations; visit hospital morgues and admissions; coordinate consular access for family members arriving in Kathmandu; assist with emergency passports and body repatriation documentation.

  2. 2. What embassies CANNOT do

    Deploy private helicopters or rescue dogs themselves; override Nepalese law or hospital medical decisions; pay for private search operations out of state funds.

  3. 3. Consular Support Desk location in Kathmandu

    A joint multi-national Consular Support Desk is operating at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA), Arrivals Hall Desk 4 (open 08:00 – 20:00). Direct phone: +977 1 4000113.

  4. 4. Information to provide your embassy

    Passport number and copy; travel insurance details and emergency hotline; travel agency/tour operator name; last known GPS track or itinerary; and your FamilyConnect case reference number.
