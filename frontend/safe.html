I am safe

Use this if you are the person affected and you are safe. We only ask for what is needed to match you to people who are looking for you.

A town, camp, hotel or landmark is enough.
Phone number, email, or the name of someone with you.
This may be shared with family who are authorised on your case.

Before you submit

  • Only declare safety for yourself, or for someone present who asks you to.
  • A declaration is recorded as reported by you until a coordinator checks it.
  • Being related to someone does not by itself grant access to their case.

Need help instead?

If you need shelter, transport, medical care or documents, submit this first, then ask for assistance from your case tracking page.

FamilyConnect coordinates information and assistance. It is not a police, medical, forensic or consular authority and does not replace them.

In an emergency contact the local emergency services. Information shown here always carries its source and verification state. Unverified information is never a confirmation.

Translations are provided to help you understand. Where a translation and the English record differ, coordinators work from the original record, which is language-neutral.
