Safe water points

Verified safe drinking water sources, distribution points, and purification stations.

Never drink untreated water after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, viruses, and chemical contaminants. Use only verified water points or purify water by boiling for at least 1 minute.

This is a public service humanitarian site.
