Arriving in the affected region? 3 Priority Actions

  1. Register Case Reference: Obtain a reference before moving across physical facilities (avoids repeated intakes).
  2. Assistance Centre Navigation: Visit designated physical hubs for translation, lodging, and consular coordination.
  3. Log Family Location: Update your in-city residence (hotel/shelter) so caseworkers can coordinate transport.
